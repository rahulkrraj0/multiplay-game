Optional Snakes & Ladders sound files

No audio files are currently included in this folder. The game uses synthesized
sound effects until matching files are added; these filenames are discovered
automatically and replace the synthesized cue when available.

- roll.ogg - dice roll
- step.ogg - token moves across the board
- snake-slide.ogg - token slides down a snake
- ladder-climb.ogg - token climbs a ladder
